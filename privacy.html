Privacy Policy

Clip That Uploader · Last updated 13 November 2026

Who this applies to

Clip That Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Clip That to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the tool uses these YouTube API scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload and schedule videos on the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the owner's channel and video details.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read view counts and other statistics for the owner's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish videos to the owner's channel and to decide which kinds of videos to make next. Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and no data is used for advertising.

Clip That Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's data folder on the owner's computer removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Clip That YouTube channel.
